# 10. Bank / Cash Tests (Finance Authorizer)

## 10.1 Bank Accounts
Open Bank Accounts and confirm you can see the test bank/cash accounts and their current balances.

## 10.2 Bank / Cash Transfer
Transfer a small test amount from one test bank/cash account to another. Confirm both balances update correctly.

## 10.3 A Receipt Into a Specific Account
Record a customer receipt into a specific test bank or cash account (not the default). Confirm that account's balance increases by the right amount.

## 10.4 Bank Reconciliation
If a test bank statement is available, try matching it against posted transactions.
