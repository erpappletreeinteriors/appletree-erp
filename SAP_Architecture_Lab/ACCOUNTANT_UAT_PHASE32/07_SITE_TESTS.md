# 7. Site Tests (Project Manager / Purchase User)

## 7.1 Labour & Wages
Record labour for 2-3 different workers against your project in one submission (use "+ Add Worker"). Calculate the total cost yourself (days × rate per worker, summed) and confirm it matches what the system shows.

## 7.2 Project Expenses
Record a small site expense (e.g. "Site Consumables," ₹2,000) against your project.

## 7.3 Tasks, Timesheet, Risk Register
Log a task, a timesheet entry, and a risk against your project. These should NOT create any accounting entries — they're for tracking only.

## 7.4 Weekly Scorecard
Capture a snapshot of your project's current numbers. Note this is a frozen snapshot — it won't update on its own; you capture a new one each time you want a fresh comparison point.

## 7.5 Confirm on Project 360
Go back to Project 360 and confirm your labour and expenses are correctly included in the Actual Cost figure.
