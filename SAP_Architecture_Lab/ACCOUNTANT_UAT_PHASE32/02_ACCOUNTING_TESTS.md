# 2. Accounting Tests (Accountant / Finance Authorizer)

## 2.1 New Journal Voucher
Post a small test journal entry (e.g. ₹100, two lines that balance). Confirm you can enter Document Date, Posting Date, a Reference, Debit, Credit, and Narration.

## 2.2 Document Workflow
Submit your entry, then log in as a different user to Approve it, then Post it. Confirm you cannot approve your own entry while logged in as the same user who created it.

## 2.3 Journal Register
Find your posted entry in the Journal Register.

## 2.4 Document Viewer
Open the same entry in Document Viewer. Confirm you can clearly see: Voucher Number, Dates, Account, Debit, Credit, Project (if any), Party (if any), and that Total Debit equals Total Credit.

## 2.5 General Ledger
Open General Ledger, filter by the account you just posted to, and confirm your entry appears with a running balance.

## 2.6 Reverse It
Reverse your test entry from the Journal Register. Confirm a new, opposite entry appears and the original is marked reversed.

## 2.7 Trial Balance
Open Trial Balance and confirm total debits equal total credits for the whole company.
