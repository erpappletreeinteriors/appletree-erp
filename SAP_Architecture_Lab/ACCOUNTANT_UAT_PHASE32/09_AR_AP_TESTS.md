# 9. AR / AP Tests (Accountant / Finance Authorizer)

## 9.1 AR Ageing
Open AR Ageing and confirm your test customer's outstanding invoice appears in the correct age bucket.

## 9.2 AP Ageing
Same, for a test supplier's outstanding bill.

## 9.3 Customer Ledger
Open Customer Ledger for your test customer. Confirm you see every invoice, receipt, and credit/debit note, with a running balance, and that it says the balance "MATCHES" the AR control account.

## 9.4 Supplier Ledger
Same, for a test supplier. Confirm it says "MATCHES" against the AP control account.

## 9.5 Reconciliation Screen
Open the Reconciliation screen. Confirm AR and AP both show as matching.
