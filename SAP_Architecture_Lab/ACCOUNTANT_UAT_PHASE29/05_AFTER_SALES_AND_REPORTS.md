# 5. After-Sales, Reports & Cross-Checks

## 5.1 Warranty
Create a Warranty for your project's customer. You must type a duration in months — the system will not guess one for you (this is deliberate; Appletree has not yet told the system what its standard warranty period is).

## 5.2 Service Ticket
Log a Service Ticket against the project, referencing the warranty.

## 5.3 Company-Wide Project Profitability
Open **Company Profitability**. Confirm your test project appears in the list with sensible numbers.

## 5.4 Audit Log
Open **Audit Log**. Search for your project's ID or your test customer's name and confirm you can see a trail of what was created, by whom, and when.

## 5.5 Try Something You Shouldn't Be Allowed To Do
Log in as a role that should NOT be allowed to do something (for example, a Project Manager account not assigned to your test project, trying to view its cost breakdown). Confirm you get a clear "not authorized" message, not an error page or a crash.
