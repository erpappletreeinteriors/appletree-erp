# 4. Billing → AR → Project Profit & Loss

## 4.1 Create the Customer Invoice
Go to **Customer Invoice**. Bill your test customer for the project's contract value.

## 4.2 Receive Payment
Go to **Customer Receipt**. Record the customer paying the invoice in full. Confirm it clears.

## 4.3 Open Project 360 and Check the Numbers
With everything from Parts 1-4 now done, open **Project 360** for your project and check:
- Revenue matches what you invoiced.
- Cost includes material, labour, AND any project expenses you entered — add them up yourself on paper first, then compare.
- Profit = Revenue minus Cost, and the margin percentage looks right.

**Important:** if the "Actual Cost" number near the top of the page and the "Actual Cost" number further down in the "Cost" section don't match, please write down BOTH numbers exactly — this is a known issue being tracked, and your exact numbers help confirm it.

## 4.4 Document Viewer
Go to **Document Viewer**. Look up the Customer Invoice's accounting entry by its ID. Confirm you can clearly see: Debit, Credit, Project, Date, Party (customer), and that Total Debit = Total Credit.

## 4.5 Trial Balance / Reconciliation
Open **Trial Balance** — confirm total debits equal total credits for the whole company. Open **Reconciliation** — confirm AR and AP both show "Matches."

## 4.6 Bank / Cash
If your test project used a specific bank or cash account, open **Bank Accounts** and confirm the balance moved correctly.
