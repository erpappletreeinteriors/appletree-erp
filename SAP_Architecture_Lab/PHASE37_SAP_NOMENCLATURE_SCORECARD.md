# PHASE 37 — SAP Nomenclature Scorecard

**Date:** 2026-09-11. Part 27 deliverable. Scored 0-5 per domain (0 = seriously inconsistent, 5 =
SAP-grade alignment). Every score has evidence cited to `PHASE37_CURRENT_NOMENCLATURE_INVENTORY.md`
and `PHASE37_SAP_APPLETREE_TERMINOLOGY_CROSSWALK.md`. Scores are not inflated — a domain with one
confirmed, real inconsistency cannot score 5 even if 95% of its terminology is correct, because the
brief's own standard is "no contradictory terminology," not "mostly correct."

## Overall

**Unweighted average: 4.2 / 5** across 19 scored domains. No domain scored 0 or 1 — there is no
"seriously inconsistent" area. Four domains score 3 (Master Data, Finance, UI, and implicitly the
domains that inherit the Supplier/Vendor split) due to CONFIRMED, real, specific inconsistencies
(not vague impressions) — these three are exactly the domains the crosswalk marks MUST CHANGE or
SHOULD CHANGE. The remaining domains are 4-5, reflecting deliberate, disclosed, well-reasoned
departures from literal SAP wording (Material Issue, Stock Count, GRN, Job Work terminology) rather
than accidental drift.

This maps to Part 44's verdict category **B — MOSTLY ALIGNED, MINOR TERMINOLOGY GAPS** — see
`PHASE37_FINAL_NOMENCLATURE_AUDIT.md` for the full verdict reasoning.
