# APPLETREE ERP — MONTH-END CLOSING CHECKLIST
*(UAT / Demo version. This is a RECOMMENDED procedure built from what the ERP actually supports today — Management/Finance confirmation is required before treating this as Apple Tree's official month-end process.)*

## Before you start

- [ ] Confirm the Financial Period for the month exists (FINANCE → Financial Periods). If not, create it.
- [ ] Tell all departments the period is closing soon, so pending drafts get submitted in time.

## Step 1 — Clear the backlog *(Recommended — confirmation required)*

- [ ] No Draft documents left sitting unsubmitted from the month (Journal Vouchers, Purchase Requisitions, Purchase Orders, Site MRS).
- [ ] No Submitted documents left waiting on an approver — chase them down.
- [ ] All GRNs for the month recorded — nothing physically received but not yet entered.

## Step 2 — Review Journal Entries *(Recommended — confirmation required)*

- [ ] Review the Journal Register for the month — anything unusual or manually entered that needs a second look.
- [ ] Confirm no journal entry is still sitting in Draft/Submitted/Approved without being Posted.

## Step 3 — AR Review

- [ ] Run AR Ageing. Follow up on anything significantly overdue.
- [ ] Confirm every Customer Receipt this month has been Cleared against the right invoice.

## Step 4 — AP Review

- [ ] Run AP Ageing. Confirm nothing critical is overdue to a supplier.
- [ ] Confirm every Supplier Payment this month has been Cleared against the right bill.
- [ ] Confirm no bill is sitting with an unresolved three-way-match exception.

## Step 5 — Inventory Reconciliation

- [ ] Run the Reconciliation screen — confirm computed Inventory value matches the GL Inventory account balance.
- [ ] Review the Site Material Reconciliation for every active site — investigate any variance beyond tolerance.
- [ ] Review open Job Work Orders — confirm nothing has quietly gone overdue on its return period without an approved extension.

## Step 6 — Bank Reconciliation

- [ ] Import/match the real bank statement for every bank account.
- [ ] Confirm no unmatched lines remain unexplained.

## Step 7 — Fixed Assets

- [ ] Confirm depreciation has been posted for the month on every capitalized asset.
- [ ] Run the Fixed Asset Reconciliation — sum of individual assets matches the GL control account.

## Step 8 — Tax Review

- [ ] Review TDS deductions for the month (FINANCE SOP → Compliance Reports) — confirm every threshold-crossing payment was correctly categorized.
- [ ] Review the ITC Reversal report for the month.
- [ ] Review the Seller Cumulative / 194Q report for any supplier newly crossing the ₹50 lakh threshold.

## Step 9 — Project Cost Review

- [ ] Spot-check a sample of active projects' Actual Cost vs. budget on Project 360.
- [ ] Review the BOQ/BOM Variance report for any project flagged with abnormal consumption.

## Step 10 — Exception Review

- [ ] Review the Cash Control Exceptions report for the month — confirm every override had a genuine, documented reason.
- [ ] Review the SOP Compliance Dashboard — nothing newly added to Configuration Required / Management Decisions that hasn't been flagged upward.

## Step 11 — Close the Period

- [ ] Once everything above is clean, close the Financial Period (FINANCE → Financial Periods). This blocks further posting into that period unless an authorized override role is configured.

## Step 12 — Reports Pack

- [ ] Generate/export: Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, Company P&L, AR Ageing, AP Ageing, Reconciliation summary.

---

**Reminder:** every step marked in this checklist reflects what the ERP is technically capable of supporting today — it is not yet an approved, mandatory Apple Tree company procedure until Finance/Management formally confirms it.
