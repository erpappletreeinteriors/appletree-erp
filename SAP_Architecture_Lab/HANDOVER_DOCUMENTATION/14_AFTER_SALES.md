# 14 — After-Sales

## Flow
Complaint → classified as Warranty or Chargeable → Service Ticket → Service Visit → Diagnosis (high-value or disputed diagnoses require a second person's approval — never the same technician who diagnosed it) → Material/Labour recorded → for Chargeable work, a real Customer Invoice is drafted through the same billing engine as every other invoice.

## Customer 360
One screen per customer showing every Warranty, Complaint, Service Ticket, Service Visit, and AMC Contract they have, plus their outstanding AR — the customer-facing service history in one place.

## SLA tracking
Response SLA = 4 hours, Site Visit SLA = 72 hours from the same start clock (both approved policy — Phase 13). **Resolution time is explicitly NOT SLA-tracked** — this was a deliberate management decision, not an oversight; do not assume 72 hours means the issue is resolved, only that a site visit occurred.

## Threshold for chargeable approval
Any chargeable diagnosis at or above ₹10,000, or any disputed diagnosis, requires approval from someone other than the diagnosing technician before it can be billed (approved policy, Phase 13).
