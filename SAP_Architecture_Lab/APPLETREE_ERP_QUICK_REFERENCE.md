## Status meanings (quick glance)

Draft = being prepared · Submitted = sent for approval · Approved = ready for next step · Rejected = sent back, read the reason · Posted = permanently in the books · Cancelled = stopped before any effect · Reversed = formally undone by a new opposite entry · Cleared = matched against its invoice, no longer outstanding.
